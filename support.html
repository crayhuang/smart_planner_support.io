Frequently asked questions

Why is full Calendar and Reminders access required?

Smart Planner displays and manages existing events and tasks. Full access is required to read, create, edit, complete, delete and synchronize these items.

I denied permission. How do I enable it again?

Open iPhone Settings, find Smart Planner, then enable full access for Calendars and Reminders. Return to the app and reopen the permissions section if the status does not update immediately.

Why did my widget not update immediately?

Smart Planner requests widget reloads after changes, but iOS controls the final refresh timing. Open the app once and confirm Calendar and Reminders permissions are still enabled.

What are “Unscheduled” todos?

These are incomplete reminders without a due date. Use the Unscheduled entry in Calendar to assign dates, edit or delete them.

How do I manage my subscription?

Use Mine → Subscription in Smart Planner, or open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

How is AI data handled?

AI requests may send your input and the relevant schedule context required for the planning or summary you requested. See the privacy policy for details.

Troubleshooting

Events or todos are missing

  • Check Calendar and Reminders permissions.
  • Open Calendar list selection and enable the required lists.
  • Pull to refresh or use the refresh button.

AI parsing fails

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Try a shorter, more explicit sentence.
  • Confirm your monthly usage or subscription status.
