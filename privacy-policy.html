Introduction

Welcome to Smart Planner. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Key Point: Smart Planner is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share your personal calendar data or reminders.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Calendar Data: We do not read, store, or access your existing calendar events
  • Reminder Data: We do not read, store, or access your existing reminders
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Location Data: We do not track your physical location
  • Contacts: We do not access your contact list

Information We Process Temporarily

  • Text Input: Your natural language input is sent to our AI service (DeepSeek) for parsing. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored permanently
  • Usage Statistics: We track the number of AI parses you've used to enforce subscription limits. This is stored locally on your device

How We Use Your Information

AI Parsing

When you submit text for AI parsing:

  • Your input is sent to DeepSeek API for natural language processing
  • The AI extracts events and tasks from your text
  • Results are returned to your device
  • No data is stored on our servers

Calendar and Reminder Creation

When you save parsed results:

  • Events are created directly in your Apple Calendar
  • Tasks are created directly in your Apple Reminders
  • All data stays on your device and in your iCloud (if enabled)
  • We never access this data after creation

Permissions We Request

Calendar Access

Why we need it: To create calendar events based on your input

What we do: Write-only access to create events

What we don't do: Read your existing calendar data

Reminders Access

Why we need it: To create reminder tasks based on your input

What we do: Write-only access to create reminders

What we don't do: Read your existing reminders

Important: Without these permissions, Smart Planner cannot function. However, we only use them to create new items when you explicitly request it.

Third-Party Services

DeepSeek AI

We use DeepSeek's AI service to parse your natural language input. When you use the AI parsing feature:

  • Your text input is sent to DeepSeek's servers
  • DeepSeek processes the text and returns structured data
  • DeepSeek's privacy policy applies to this processing
  • Learn more: DeepSeek Privacy Policy

Apple Services

Smart Planner integrates with Apple's native Calendar and Reminders apps:

  • All data is stored using Apple's infrastructure
  • If you use iCloud, your data syncs according to Apple's privacy policy
  • Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple:

  • We do not handle payment information
  • Apple manages all billing and payment data
  • We only receive confirmation of subscription status

Data Security

How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption: All data transmitted to DeepSeek API uses HTTPS encryption
  • No Storage: We do not store your calendar data or reminders on any server
  • Local Processing: Subscription status and usage counts are stored locally on your device
  • Minimal Data: We only process the minimum data necessary for functionality
Your Control: You can delete the app at any time to remove all local data. Your calendar events and reminders will remain in Apple's Calendar and Reminders apps.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Review what data is stored locally on your device
  • Delete: Remove the app to delete all local data
  • Revoke Permissions: Disable Calendar and Reminders access in iOS Settings at any time
  • Cancel Subscription: Cancel your subscription through Apple's subscription management
  • Contact Us: Ask questions about your privacy at any time

Children's Privacy

Smart Planner is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new privacy policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Notifying you through the app (for significant changes)

You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.
